# Aditi Super App — Privacy Policy & Data Protections

## 1. User Consent & Privacy Controls
- **Location Sharing**: Explicit user permission required. Automatic time-bound expiration for live locations.
- **Matrimony & Dating**: Sensitive astrological and birth information visibility controlled via user settings.
- **Account Deletion & Data Portability**: Users have full rights to request complete deletion of their profiles and all associated relational records (`auth.users(id)` CASCADE).
