# Assessment Engine, Mock Simulation & Mistake Remediation

## 1. ICAI Assessment Policy

- **Subjective Marking (Papers 1 & 2)**:
  - Law: Step-marks evaluated across Statutory Provision (2M), Relevant Landmark Case Precedent (1.5M), Application to Facts (1.5M), and Final Conclusion (1M).
  - Accounting: Assessed on Correct Journal Entry Accounts, Working Notes, and Balances.
- **Objective Negative Marking (Papers 3 & 4)**:
  - Correct answer: +1.00 Mark.
  - Wrong answer: -0.25 Mark penalty.
  - Unattempted: 0.00 Marks.

---

## 2. "My Mistakes" Auto-Notebook

- Every wrong answer in practice or mock exams is classified into:
  - `CONCEPTUAL`: Fundamental theoretical misunderstanding.
  - `CALCULATION`: Arithmetic or calculator shortcut error.
  - `CARELESS`: Missed keyword or easy question lost.
  - `FORMULA`: Wrong formula or sign applied.
- Includes 1-click **"Fix My Mistakes with AI Tutor"** button to trigger remediation sessions.
