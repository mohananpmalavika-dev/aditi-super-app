# Adaptive Study Planning, Spaced Repetition & Exam Readiness

## 1. Adaptive Study Planning & Rebalancing

- **74-Day Exam Countdown**: Generates hourly target study blocks based on student daily availability (e.g. 3.5 hrs/day).
- **Non-Punitive Plan Rebalancing**: When a student misses study days or hours, the engine reallocates remaining chapters across remaining days without failing the plan.

---

## 2. Spaced Repetition Intervals

Retention is reinforced at scientifically validated intervals:
- **Day 0**: Initial lesson completion.
- **Day 1**: 24-hour recall.
- **Day 3**: 72-hour recall.
- **Day 7**: 1-week revision.
- **Day 14**: 2-week consolidation.
- **Day 30**: 1-month mastery check.

If a concept is answered incorrectly, its interval resets to **Day 1** for immediate reinforcement.

---

## 3. Exam Readiness Score (0–100) & Predicted Marks

- Evaluates completion rate, practice accuracy, revision adherence, and mock exam scores.
- Predicts expected mark range (e.g. `235 – 268 / 400`) to give students actionable confidence without false certainty.
