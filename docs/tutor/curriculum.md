# CA Foundation Curriculum & Academic Content Standards

## 1. Curriculum Versions

The platform isolates all syllabus definitions by `CurriculumVersionId`:
- `CA_FOUNDATION_SEP_2026`: 74-day countdown examination version.
- `CA_FOUNDATION_JAN_2027`: Winter session.
- `CA_FOUNDATION_MAY_2027`: Spring session.
- `CA_INTERMEDIATE_NOV_2026`: Extensible track for Group 1 & 2.
- `CA_FINAL_MAY_2027`: Final examination track.

---

## 2. Four CA Foundation Papers

| Paper | Title | Marks | Pattern | Negative Marking | Key Competencies |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Paper 1** | Accounting | 100 | Subjective | None | Theoretical framework, Journal Entries, Ledger, BRS, Depreciation, Consignment |
| **Paper 2** | Business Laws | 100 | Subjective | None | Indian Contract Act 1872, Sale of Goods Act 1930, Indian Partnership Act 1932, LLP Act 2008, Companies Act 2013 |
| **Paper 3** | Quantitative Aptitude | 100 | Objective MCQ | -0.25 Marks | Mathematics of Finance, Annuity, Logical Reasoning, Statistics, Probability |
| **Paper 4** | Business Economics | 100 | Objective MCQ | -0.25 Marks | Theory of Demand/Supply, Consumer Behaviour, Production & Cost, Price Determination |

## 3. Bilingual Terminology & Translation Safeguards

To maintain ICAI academic rigor, technical accounting and legal keywords are flagged with `doNotTranslate: true`:
- Accounting: `Bank A/c Dr`, `Pass Book`, `Cash Book`, `Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS)`, `Trial Balance`, `Carriage Inwards`.
- Law: `Consideration`, `Quid Pro Quo`, `Stranger to Contract`, `Chinnaya v. Ramayya`, `Privity of Contract`, `Free Consent`, `Coercion`.
- Quantitative: `Annuity Regular`, `Annuity Due`, `Present Value (PV)`, `Future Value (FV)`, `Perpetuity`.
- Economics: `Price Elasticity of Demand (Ep)`, `Total Outlay Method`, `Marginal Utility`, `Equilibrium Price`.
